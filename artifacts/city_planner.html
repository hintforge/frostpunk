Off-grid & on-site buildings
Mines, drills, steelworks and thumpers sit on a deposit, not in a ring — they cost resources and still need heat from a Steam Hub, but they never eat ring space. Steam Hubs, Shrines and Agitators are 1×1 on a road and are treated the same way.

Calibration derived, not published

None of these four numbers is published anywhere — not on the wiki, not in the game's own UI. They are derived: ring capacity is the circumference in tiles at the ring's mid-radius, and the base heat radius is back-solved from a community measurement of how many tents one range upgrade adds. The model runs up to ~13% pessimistic on outer rings against the only published measurement that exists, which is itself internally inconsistent. If the game fits more than the planner says, the game is right — change the numbers here.
Ring capacity is the circumference in tiles at the ring's mid-radius, round(2π · r). Only +3 tiles per Generator Range Upgrade comes from the corpus (items/upgrades.md, confidence confirmed); the rest of this panel is inference.
Where these numbers come from
Footprints, build costs, insulation and unlock requirements — Official Frostpunk Wiki Buildings table, captured 2026-09-16. Footprints are angular × radial tiles: the first number is width along the curve, the second is depth straight out from the generator. The wiki states buildings "may slightly shrink or grow in size to accommodate their surroundings", so no planner can be exact.

Research costs and prerequisites — this guide's own items/upgrades.md, research-integrated 2026-06-03. Build cost and research cost are different numbers and are shown separately.

Ring capacity, base heat radius and Steam Hub radius — derived. See the Model tab.

Not modelled: roads and road adjacency, shelter population capacity (unsourced), coal burn per ring, The Last Autumn and On The Edge (both replace the generator heating line with Braziers).
