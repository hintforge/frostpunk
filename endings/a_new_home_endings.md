## Victory branches

A New Home is won by surviving the **Great Storm** (the final 7-day endgame event). The *character* of the victory is judged by the laws you signed and the deaths you allowed:

- **The Saviour (humanitarian ending):** survive while staying on the humane side -- stop before the Purpose-tree "crossing the line" laws (Faith: Protector of the Truth / Righteous Denunciation; Order: Pledge of Loyalty / Forceful Persuasion) and avoid mass deaths. Achievement-linked.
- **The Iron Saviour (authoritarian ending):** survive having signed the terminal Purpose law (**New Faith** or **New Order**), which removes Hope and auto-builds an Execution Platform. Achievement-linked; mutually exclusive in spirit with the Saviour path.

## The "Golden Path"
A distinct achievement (not an ending per se): finish A New Home **without** severe laws / harsh abilities, **without** a Needs Crisis, and **without** forbidden deaths. Most Adaptation laws (incl. Soup, Extended Shift) are safe; the failures come from Triage, the Emergency Shift ability, citizen deaths, or Needs Crises. Hidden achievement; global unlock rate is low (Steam ~1.9%; Exophase ~3.80%).
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (Steam guide + Fandom Golden Path page) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-004, PON-002: Golden Path interacts with both the Law system (crossing-the-line Purpose laws block it; most Adaptation laws are safe) and the ending system; see dependencies.md for the full condition list.

## Failure conditions
- **Hope collapse** (Hope reaches minimum) → the population exiles the Captain / abandons the city.
- **Discontent revolt** (Discontent reaches maximum) → uprising ends the run.
- **Mass death / population loss** from cold or starvation.
