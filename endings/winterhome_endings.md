## The scenario's core turn (late-game)
You begin with a pre-built, ruined Winterhome and restore it -- only to discover the **Generator is irreparably damaged.** It suffers escalating malfunctions/stress (engineers assigned to it buy time). **The city cannot be saved; the only victory is evacuation.**
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (4 sources, 2 languages) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_

## Evacuation (the win condition)
Build an **Evacuation Centre** and retrofit the **Land Dreadnought.** Fully upgrading the Dreadnought costs **5000 Coal, 5000 Food Rations, 9000 Steel, and 10 Steam Cores**, giving capacity for up to **500 people.** The **mandatory gate is 5000 Coal** ("Once 5000 Coal is supplied, the Dreadnought can be manually launched"); the other three are proportional **optional** objectives that increase how many people you save. The Dreadnought auto-launches when the Generator finally explodes (~13 days to prepare after the evacuation trigger).
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` PON-003, SEQ-001: the 9,000-Steel requirement makes the Steel Bridge automaton choice and Advanced Steelworks research priority the two critical gates for reaching the full Dreadnought.

## Achievement-linked outcomes
- **Winterhome** -- finish the scenario.
- **Full Dreadnought** -- fully upgrade *and* fill the Dreadnought (by launch).
- **All children on board** -- evacuate all children (by launch).

## Failure conditions
Generator explodes before the Dreadnought meets its mandatory 5000-Coal gate; standard Hope/Discontent collapse.
