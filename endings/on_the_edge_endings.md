## Two primary endings (binary final choice) `spoiler: late-game`

Determined by the final **Save vs Let New London Fall** decision.

### Save New London

Send Coal, Workers, building materials (Steel + Steam Cores), and Food in a 5-day window. Loyal allies auto-send Coal / Workers / Food.

**Ending text variants (player-reported):** `spoiler: story`
- "United Territories of New London" -- inferred when multiple settlements were loyal
- "New London Territory" -- inferred when fewer settlements were loyal

Note: exact text-variant trigger (specific number of loyal settlements) is player-inferred, not officially documented.
_source: Steam discussion on ending variants · confidence: medium (community-reported; not officially documented)_

Achievement: **We Are In This Together**

### Let New London Fall

Prepare for refugee waves during a 5-day window: 5 working Infirmaries, +20 Bunkhouses / Houses (built after the decision; tent upgrades count), 200 Food Rations, all housing Cold-or-warmer, no homeless.

The region reorganizes around the outpost.

Achievement: **Unforgiven** _(hidden)_

## Defeat conditions `spoiler: late-game`

- **Failing the Day-7 first shipment to New London** (if the 24h last-chance deadline is also missed) → instant game over.
- **Failing either 5-day final-act quest** (failing to supply New London or failing to prepare for refugees) → defeat.
- **Standard death spiral** (excessive Discontent, population collapse).

## PoNR gates

| Gate | Effect |
