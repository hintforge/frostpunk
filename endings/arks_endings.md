## Objective and win gate
Keep the **4 Seedling Arks** above freezing and meet your own city's completion gate by the deadline: **gather 8000 Coal, gather 500 Food Rations, build 5 houses, and disband all expeditions/outposts by the deadline.** Failing your *own* gate by the deadline loses the scenario **regardless of surplus production**.

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` SEQ-003: if completing all three New Manchester shipments (including 6,000 Coal for the 3rd), total coal needed is ≥14,000 Coal -- the two goals compete for the same stockpile.

## The New Manchester relief arc (~day 15)
An **optional** branch in which a sister-city, New Manchester, requests escalating shipments:
1. "Send 600 Steel, 600 Wood and 1 Automaton"
2. "Send 2000 Food Rations and 2 Automatons"
3. "Send 6000 Coal and 4 Automatons"

Completing all three saves New Manchester (the **best ending** -- "Everybody Lived for Once" requires saving both cities). It is optional: you can win by meeting only your own gate.
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: mainline · spoiler: story_

## Achievement-linked outcomes
- **Conservationist** -- save all 4 Seedling Arks (by the storm).
- **Everybody Lived for Once** -- finish saving both cities (by the deadline).
- **Sprinter** (hidden) -- reach/find New Manchester before Day 15.
- **Technocrat** -- finish the scenario.
