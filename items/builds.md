# Frostpunk -- Builds (Law Path + Tech Priority Combinations)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02

Strategic "build" choices in Frostpunk arise from two interacting decision axes:
1. **Law path:** Faith tree vs Order tree (Purpose laws, mutually exclusive) -- or Adaptation-only.
2. **Technology priority:** which tabs and nodes to research first.

Build recommendations are interpretation/advice, not falsifiable claims -- they're presented as community consensus with disagreements flagged. The underlying mechanics they rest on are claimed in `mechanics.md`, `paths/`, and `items/upgrades.md`.

---

## Law paths overview

**status:** research-integrated

The Book of Laws has two trees: **Adaptation** (from start, problem-solving) and **Purpose** (unlocked mid-scenario, choose ONE of Faith or Order -- mutually exclusive and permanent). Purpose's job is to raise Hope / lower Discontent.

- **Faith path:** leverages collective belief for Hope boosts; stronger Hope tools.
- **Order path:** leverages surveillance and discipline for productivity; the Hope bar becomes Obedience.
- **Adaptation-only (no Purpose):** viable; the player manages Hope/Discontent via promises and buildings instead of a Purpose tree.

See `paths/` for full per-law effects and ending consequences (spoiler-tier gated).

## Recommended builds (per scenario)

**status:** research-integrated

### A New Home -- builds

- **Faith build (best for Hope / Golden Path):** Adaptation core (Extended Shift, Child Shelters → Medic Apprentices, Soup, Sustain Life → Care House, Cemetery → Ceremonial Funerals) + Faith (House of Prayer → Evening Prayers → Faith Keepers; Shrines → House of Healing). **Stop before Protector of the Truth** to keep the good ending. Mainstream consensus: Faith is easier for the Londoners crisis because its Hope tools are stronger.
  _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (4 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_
- **Order build (best for Discontent / productivity):** Adaptation core + Order (Neighbourhood Watch → Guard Stations → Patrol; Propaganda Centre → Agitator). Best when Discontent is the dominant failure mode; Foreman/Agitator boost workshop research. **Stop before Pledge of Loyalty / Forceful Persuasion** for the good ending.
  _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_
- **Adaptation-only (no Purpose):** viable for the **Golden Path** achievement -- most Adaptation laws are safe (incl. Extended Shift, Organ Transplants, Overcrowding, Prosthetics). **Soup does NOT fail Golden Path** (a common myth -- community-verified false). Golden Path failures come from harsh *abilities* (Triage / the Emergency Shift ability) or from citizen deaths / Needs Crises, not from any single Adaptation law.
  _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (Steam guide + Fandom Golden Path page, resolved across sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

### The Refugees -- builds

- **Child Labour (Safe Jobs) + Overcrowding** strongly recommended to handle the sick-refugee waves and the child influx. Sick refugees are not auto-replaced when workers fall ill, so medical capacity and housing are the binding constraints.
  _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (2 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

### The Arks -- builds

- Law choices are constrained: **no Hunters** (food only via Hothouse), automaton-centric. **Sign Food Additives early** (sawdust meals count the same for stockpile + the New Manchester shipment). First tech: **Faster Gathering → Beacon**; then rush **Factory plans → Engineer Automatons**. Research throughput, not heat, is the win condition here.
  _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

### The Fall of Winterhome -- builds

- **Research Advanced Steelworks before any other advanced building** (the Dreadnought needs 9000 steel -- steel is the binding constraint). **Do not take the Steel Bridge automaton** -- it kills the steel-from-iron-deposits delivery.
  _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (2 sources, incl. RU guide) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_

### Endgame storm prep (A New Home)

- Before the Great Storm, complete **Generator Power III + Range III + Overdrive Couplings + Advanced Heaters + Safety Bypass.** The max-upgraded Infirmary is the warmest building. (See `mechanics.md` Heat System for the 100%-stress-reset trick.)

## Decision-ready guidance

- **A New Home:** default to a **Faith** Purpose path (stronger Hope tools survive the Londoners crisis); switch to **Order** only if Discontent is your dominant failure mode.
- **The Fall of Winterhome:** Advanced Steelworks first; never take the Steel Bridge automaton.
- **The Arks:** rush Engineer Automatons above all else.
