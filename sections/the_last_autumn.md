## Overview

Prequel scenario set before the freeze; the player manages an IEC construction camp building the Generator that will shelter Liverpool's people. Released January 21, 2020. Instead of the usual −20°C start, this scenario begins at a toasty **+10°C** -- warmth is available throughout until the freeze arrives around Day 27.

## Unique mechanics

_source: Deep Research P3 handoff 2026-06-03 (fandom, PSNProfiles, Neoseeker) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression · dlc: the_last_autumn_

- **Motivation** (replaces Hope) -- 3 bands giving −30% / +0% / +30% productivity. Motivation hitting 0 does NOT end the game (unlike Hope). Core resource-production modifier.
- **Strikes** -- occur only at Generator-related workplaces; triggered by low Motivation, low Work Safety, or scripted events; work stops until demands are met.
- **Work Safety / Toxic Gas** -- construction sites have a Toxic Gas parameter (0 to −3, shifts ±1 at midnight). Ventilation Plants (coal-powered) raise safety. Permanently zeroed once the Generator Core is installed.
- **Construction phases** -- the Generator must be built in 4 sequential stages, each requiring manufactured intermediate components (see milestone sequence).
- **Convicts** -- scenario-unique social class; cannot strike; eat less; work only under supervision; half the Telegraph cost of Workers. Efficiency can be pushed at the cost of safety.
- **Worker vs Engineer faction choice** -- after the first accident and strike, the player permanently sides with Engineers (→ Factory Inspectorate) or Workers (→ Labour Union). Unlocks mutually exclusive Labour law branches.
- **Foraging teams** (replaces expedition scouts) -- each needs its own tent; scouts points AND sets up food camps; moves faster than base-game scouts (no snow).
- **Telegraph Station** -- orders Workers, Engineers, Convicts, Steam Cores (20 Logistical Capacity each), Prostheses; capacity grows ~25/day to a max of 99; shipments take 24h; only one in transit at a time.
- **No Automatons** -- not buildable in this scenario.

### Law books

- **Administration** (analogous to Adaptation) -- Bath House, Medical, Public House, Hearty Meals, Cocaine Pills, House of Pleasure, On-Site Burials / Repatriation of Bodies. No child-labour laws (no children in camp).
- **Labour** (unique to TLA) -- Generator construction, work safety, shift length. Locked until Generator construction begins (Profiles Manufacture + first build action) and the first strike occurs.
- **Faith/Order trees:** ABSENT.

### Technology tree

- **Heating tab** -- uses Heaters/Braziers (no Generator upgrade line; you are building the Generator).
- **Resources / Food-Health tabs** -- modified; adds Docks, Reloading Stations, Foragers, Fishing Harbour.
- **Generator Construction tab** (replaces Exploration & Industry) -- Profiles Manufacture, Foundry, Machine Shop, Ventilation Plant.

## Milestone sequence

_source: Deep Research P3 handoff 2026-06-03 (fandom Quests, PSNProfiles, Neoseeker) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression · dlc: the_last_autumn_

1. **Basic Facilities (Day 1–2).** Research + build Docks; begin harvesting dock piles; build Fishing Harbour or set up a Forager's Camp; build Telegraph Station and request people/supplies. Entry: starting Workers gather inner-circle piles. Exit: stable food + resource income. PoNR: No.

2. **Telegraph online (early).** Capacity grows ~25/day to max 99. Prices: Workers (2 cap), Engineers (4), Convicts (1), Steam Cores (20), Prostheses (3). Shipments take 24h; only one in transit. Entry: functional Dock. Exit: manpower scaling. PoNR: No.

3. **Sign first Administration laws.** Hearty Meals → Overcrowding / Extra Rations → Chapel etc. Each law branch is mutually exclusive with its alternative. PoNR: No (but individual law choices lock alternatives permanently).

4. **Begin Generator construction → Labour book unlocks.** Entry: Profiles Manufacture researched + 8 Structural Profiles stockpiled. Exit: Labour law tree available. PoNR: No.

5. **Stage 1 — Shaft Structure (8 Structural Profiles).** First scripted disaster occurs. PoNR: No.

6. **First accident → mandatory strike → Worker/Engineer choice.** Side with Engineers → Factory Inspectorate (safety bonus, Internal Promotion) or Workers → Labour Union. **The law is force-signed and the choice is permanent.** PoNR: **YES.** `spoiler: progression`
   - This gates "All Along the Watchtower" (convicts + engineers only) and "Arise Ye Workers" (workers only) achievements.
   > **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` PON-008: the Worker/Engineer choice permanently locks the opposing Labour law branch and closes one achievement gate per run (Watchtower vs Arise Ye Workers).

7. **Stage 2 — Foundations (10 Structural Profiles + 10 Steam Exchangers).** Scripted disaster #2 (pre-warned; build safety gear to avoid deaths). PoNR: No.

8. **Foraging arc — Lost Crates / Winterhome convoy.** Foragers find crates (10 Steel Composites + 20 Steam Exchangers) north of Site 113 after scouting Bald Hill. Choice: keep them (→ achievement "It Was Me All Along") or return them to the Winterhome convoy. **If you return to base with them before meeting the convoy you can no longer return them.** PoNR: choice-locked once you bring the crates back to base. `spoiler: story`

9. **Stage 3 — Heart of the Generator / Install the Core (25 Steel Composites + 50 Structural Profiles).** Scripted disaster #3 (~68% — cave-in): detonate (−40% progress) vs let workers die vs send rescue team (only available if Work Safety ≥ Unhealthy). **Installing the Core permanently zeroes Toxic Gas.** PoNR: No (consequences permanent). `spoiler: late-game`

10. **Temperature begins dropping (Day 27, 20:00).** Schedule: D27 0°C → D31 −10 → D35 −20 → D37 −30 → D39 −40, then −50/−60 by difficulty. `spoiler: late-game`

11. **Stage 4 — Assemble the Generator (50 Steam Exchangers + 20 Steel Composites).** Final construction stage = win condition. PoNR: No. `spoiler: late-game`

12. **Day-37 "last shipment" gate.** Sea freezes on Day 37; Docks and Fishing Harbour unusable; Telegraph stops accepting orders; coal only via Charcoal Kiln, wood only via Sawmills, steel from the Shipwreck. **If all four construction stages are complete by Day 37, you may LEAVE IMMEDIATELY** (achievement "I'll Be Home for Christmas"). PoNR: **YES** (logistics permanently locked). `spoiler: late-game`
    > **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` PON-007: the Day-37 sea freeze permanently locks all supply logistics (Docks, Telegraph); plan all resource orders and stage completions before this gate.

13. **Optional: 3 Generator upgrades + 2 safety precautions (any time before departure).** No gameplay effect on run outcome; changes ending text. Required for "Perfectionist" achievement. PoNR: No (must be done before leaving). `spoiler: progression`

14. **The Final Days (to Day 45).** If you stay after Day 37 or aren't done, survive the freeze and wait for the icebreaker to evacuate everyone. Victory condition = icebreaker arrives with everyone alive. PoNR: No. `spoiler: late-game`

**Total Generator material cost** (all stages + 3 upgrades + 2 safety precautions): 68 Structural Profiles, 110 Steam Exchangers, 70 Steel Composites (≈1700 Wood / 3950 Steel / 3500 Coal raw; ≈1360/3160/2800 with all efficiency tech).

## Missables

_source: Deep Research P3 handoff 2026-06-03 (fandom, TV Tropes, Steam, PSNProfiles) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression · dlc: the_last_autumn · missable: yes_

- **"It Was Me All Along" (achievement)** -- keep the Winterhome generator parts (Lost Crates). Lost permanently if you drop them at base before meeting the convoy. Achievement-linked: YES. `spoiler: story`
- **"I'll Be Home for Christmas" (achievement)** -- complete all 4 stages before Day-37 sea-freeze. Deadline: Day 37. Achievement-linked: YES.
- **Worker/Engineer exclusive Labour laws** -- choosing one side permanently locks the other's law set (e.g., Internal Promotion / Factory Inspectorate vs Labour Union). Gates "All Along the Watchtower" and "Arise Ye Workers." Deadline: post-first-strike choice.
- **"Perfectionist" (achievement)** -- all 3 Generator upgrades + zero construction faults; faults from failed disaster choices are permanent. Achievement-linked: YES.
- **Optional Generator upgrades** -- only buildable before departure; "complete generator" ending text variant lost if you leave early without them.

See also `sections/missables.md` for the cross-scenario aggregated list.
