# Endless Mode -- Reference

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-03 (P3)

A base-game mode (added by the free patch 1.3.0) and in P1 scope. Open-ended survival against **cyclical storms** -- the only non-scripted, sandbox mode. Uses the A New Home tech tree **minus Stereoscopic Lenses and Outpost Depot** (no Outposts in Endless). The Frostland **regenerates after each storm**, and **Relics** are collectible.

## Sub-modes

| Sub-mode | Start | Tech | Storm cadence | Temp floor | Pop cap | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Maps
**8 maps** (Crater, Flats, Canyon, Crags, Rifts, etc.), each with **3 layouts.** The **Rifts** map adds unique Bridges and starts with 5 steam cores. Steam cores are effectively unlimited at map edges (up to ~5 per far node). Pop cap ~600 is the threshold for receiving new-citizen Frostland nodes.
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (2-3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

## Achievements
**Marathon** = survive 50 days of Endurance; **Ultramarathon** = survive 100 days of Endurance. Both have three tiers: Medium-or-higher, Hard-or-higher, and Extreme. Master Archivist (collect Relics), Endless Social Activist, Endless Slave Driver, and the Serenity "lose on purpose" achievement are also Endless-mode (base game). See `achievements.md`.

## Builders DLC details

`dlc:endless_builders` · _source: Deep Research P3 handoff 2026-06-03 (fandom Endless mode, Steam discussions, 11 bit studios patch notes) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none · dlc: endless_builders_

**Builders is NOT cosmetic.** 11 bit studios' own patch notes describe it as *"the new variant of the Endless Mode – Builders – that redefines the challenges you'll face by providing different setup and solutions."* Widely described as the hardest endless mode (no heat until ~4th storm).

- Start: 125 citizens, 4 Tents, 1 Medical Post, 5 Steel Ruins, 8 Wood Ruins, 1 Generator Ruin. Temperature always −20°C.
- Steam Hubs and Automaton upgrades are unavailable until the Generator is finished (Automatons buildable but inert).
- The Generator must be rebuilt in stages using the Last Autumn construction system. **Each blizzard resets progress on the current stage in construction and demolishes unfinished stages.** Rush a stage in a few hours to add a permanent malfunction.
- Survivor shelters appear after Day 50; relics after the 2nd storm.
- **Tech + laws:** base game tech tree + laws (NOT The Last Autumn's Administration/Labour books).
- **No fixed victory condition** -- survive and rebuild indefinitely against cyclical storms.

**Builders achievements (4):**
- **A for Effort** -- build Generator with ALL construction faults *(historically buggy; was unobtainable at launch -- re-verify vs current patch)*
- **One More Day Syndrome** -- build Generator + reach Day 100
- **Winter Ready** -- build Generator before the weather changes
- **Weathering the Storm** -- build Generator with no storm damage

**Note on achievement routing:** Marathon/Ultramarathon require **Endurance** mode; Hi Marek! requires **Serenity**; Hyperefficient (>200% efficiency) is easiest via **Builders** Rush Construction. Do NOT conflate modes.
