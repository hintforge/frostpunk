## The Last Autumn (DLC)

_source: Deep Research P3 handoff 2026-06-03 (fandom, TV Tropes, Steam, PSNProfiles) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression · dlc: the_last_autumn · missable: yes_

- **"It Was Me All Along" (achievement)** -- Keep the Winterhome generator parts (Lost Crates found north of Site 113). Lost permanently if you return to base before meeting the Winterhome convoy. `spoiler: story`
- **"I'll Be Home for Christmas" (achievement)** -- Complete all 4 Generator construction stages before the Day-37 sea-freeze. Deadline: Day 37 ~18:00. After this, Docks become unusable and logistics are permanently locked.
- **Worker/Engineer exclusive Labour laws** -- Siding with one faction permanently locks the other's law set. Gates "All Along the Watchtower" (convicts+engineers) and "Arise Ye Workers" (workers). Deadline: post-first-strike choice. This is the scenario's only permanent PoNR.
- **"Perfectionist" (achievement)** -- All 3 Generator upgrades built + zero construction faults. Construction faults from failed disaster choices are permanent. Build the upgrades before departure.
- **Optional Generator upgrades** -- Only buildable before you leave. "Complete generator" ending text variant lost if you depart without them.

## On The Edge (DLC)

_source: Deep Research P3 handoff 2026-06-03 (fandom, PSNProfiles, Steam guides) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression · dlc: on_the_edge · missable: yes_

- **First shipment deadline** -- Send 100 Steel + 2 Steam Cores by Day 7 ~18:00. Miss this hard gate → "Last chance" 24h warning; if still unsent → instant game over (permanent loss). Not achievement-linked but is the run's earliest hard PoNR.
- **"I Feel Lucky" (achievement, hidden)** -- Take the risky choice at ALL THREE: Tesla City, Steel Bridge, AND Victims Colony. Missing any one of these forfeit sites voids the achievement for that run. Deadline: while sites are scoutable. `spoiler: progression`
- **Settlement-improvement achievements** (Green Thumb / Defender of the Oppressed / Guardian / Contractor) -- Complete all improvements at each settlement before scenario end. Favour mismanagement can soft-lock these per-run.
- **"Social Activist" vs "Slave Driver"** -- Mutually exclusive per run. All settlements loyal vs all distrustful. Both awarded only at run end.
- **"Frostland Explorer" / "All Your Base Are Connect To Us"** -- Explore all Frostland sites / connect all Safe Routes; both require not missing any scoutable site.
- **"We Are In This Together" vs "Unforgiven"** -- Mutually exclusive per run (Save vs Let Fall New London). Both are hard PoNR at the final 12h binary choice.
- **Law-path locks post-independence** -- Choosing e.g. Child Shelters vs Child Labor permanently locks the alternative. Sign laws before locking out options.
