# The Refugees -- Walkthrough

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02 (P2)

A separate base-game scenario (~26-30 days). Unlocks after surviving 20 days of A New Home. **Objective: save 250 people.** Has the hardest *start* in the base game (front-loaded difficulty).

## Starting conditions
Start: 20 workers, 8 engineers, 15 children, ~500 coal (Medium difficulty).
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_

**Purpose laws (Faith/Order) are available immediately at scenario start** -- unlike A New Home, no trigger gate is required. Choosing a path is permanent once the first Purpose law is signed.
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 4 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

## Structure
Refugees arrive every ~2 days: **10 refugee groups, then 3 Lord groups (~300 Lords total, in waves of ~100).** A class conflict between poor refugees and wealthy Lords drives the event chain. No Great Storm in this scenario.
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (3 sources, 2 languages; P2 confirms Lord counts) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

## Key mechanic
**Sick refugees are NOT auto-replaced** when workers fall ill -- medical capacity and housing are the binding constraints. **Child Labour (Safe Jobs) + Overcrowding** laws are near-essential to absorb the sick-refugee waves and the child influx.
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (2 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

## Steam cores
The Refugees is the **only scenario where cores appear in ground piles.** Sources: scouting + **2 Destroyed Steam Core Transports** in the starting crater (2 cores each = 4 total) + cores in starting piles.
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_

## Frostland
The Refugees has its own Frostland variant map (different nodes from A New Home). See `mechanics.md` Expedition System.

## Ordered milestone sequence (P2)

1. **Day 1 setup** *(none)* — 20 workers, 8 engineers, 15 children, ~500 coal. Sign Child Labour for +15 effective workers. Two Destroyed Steam Core Transports in the starting crater yield 2 cores each (4 total).

2. **Purpose laws open immediately** *(progression)* — Faith/Order available from the start (unlike A New Home). Pick an unrest-quelling path early; permanent once chosen. PoNR: path choice is permanent.
   _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 4 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

3. **Refugee waves** *(progression)* — Arrive roughly every 2 days; accept or refuse (refused groups return later). Drives housing and heat pressure.

4. **Sick-wave pressure** *(progression)* — Many arrivals are ill. Care House / House of Healing medical capacity, Overcrowding law, and Child Labour (Safe Jobs) are near-essential to absorb the sick-refugee waves.

5. **The Lords arrive** *(progression)* — Upper-class faction arrives in later waves (~300 Lords total, waves of ~100). Accept or refuse. Decision branches the ending. PoNR: branches ending.
   _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 4 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

6. **Lord Craven event** *(story)* — A Lord who refuses to work; notable named decision. Faith Keepers / Guard Stations allow resolution without "crossing the line." Partial PoNR.
   _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: mainline · spoiler: story_

7. **Class-conflict resolution / ending gate** *(story)* — The equality ("End the Class Struggle") ending requires sustained Hope above ~75% and Discontent below ~20% throughout the Lords conflict. The refuse-Lords branch (if Lords are barred) forces building two expensive Guarded Outposts (~500 wood / 500 steel each); failure results in the Lords massacre. Ending choices are permanent. PoNR: **YES** (determines ending).
   _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: mainline · spoiler: story_
