# The Fall of Winterhome -- Walkthrough

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02 (P2)

A separate base-game scenario (~22-26 days), shipped as a **free update** and in P1 scope. A reconstruction premise: you begin with a pre-built, ruined Winterhome. Available from the start via scenario select.

> The scenario's central turn is itself a story reveal -- most of this file is `spoiler: late-game` / `story`, gated at puzzle-tier 1.

## The core turn (late-game)
Restore the ruined city -- then discover the **Generator is irreparably damaged**, suffering escalating malfunctions/stress (engineers assigned to it buy time). **The city cannot be saved; the only victory is to evacuate** via the Land Dreadnought before the Generator explodes (~13 days to prepare after the trigger).
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (4 sources, 2 languages) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_

## Evacuation
Build an Evacuation Centre and retrofit the Land Dreadnought. Mandatory gate: **5000 Coal** to manually launch. Full-upgrade cost and capacity: `endings/winterhome_endings.md`.
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_

## Routing & timing (progression)
- Evacuate **Weather Station staff by day 10**, **Storm Shelter staff by day 15.**
- **Do NOT take the Steel Bridge automaton** -- it kills steel-from-iron-deposits delivery, and steel (9000 for the full Dreadnought) is the binding constraint. Research **Advanced Steelworks before any other advanced building.**
  _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (2 sources, RU guide) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` SEQ-001: Advanced Steelworks must be prioritized to meet the 9,000-Steel Dreadnought requirement within the ~13-day evacuation window.

## Ordered milestone sequence (P2)

1. **Day 1 inherited ruined city** *(none)* — Start: ~330 workers, 60 engineers, 190 children, 30 amputees, ~80 dead bodies. Hope near 0, Discontent near 100. Pre-signed laws (Child Labour, Public Houses, Corpse Pit) and some pre-researched tech. Purpose laws (Faith/Order) available immediately -- none pre-chosen.
   _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 4 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_

2. **Clear ruins / rebuild** *(progression)* — Clear ≥30 ruins, raise Hope to 50%, lower Discontent to 25%. Delete excess roads for wood; tear ruins from center outward to reuse generator heat.
   _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 4 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

3. **Hope 50% checkpoint (~day 10)** *(progression)* — A hard milestone. Partial PoNR -- failure to reach it by this point severely complicates the run.
   _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 2 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

4. **Steel Bridge automaton choice** *(progression)* — Taking the bridge automaton permanently blocks the Iron Deposit steel outpost. **Do NOT take it.** 9000 steel is needed for the full Dreadnought; the Iron Deposit is the binding constraint. PoNR: **YES** (missable). → `sections/missables.md`
   _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression · missable: yes_
   > **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` PON-003: taking the Steel Bridge automaton permanently blocks Iron Deposit delivery, making the 9,000-Steel Dreadnought requirement nearly unachievable.

5. **Storm Shelter / Barren Hollow scouting** *(progression)* — Scouting finds engineers required for evacuation. Missable if not reached in time.
   _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression · missable: yes_

6. **Examine the Generator (Repair Station)** *(progression)* — Research the scenario-unique Repair Station technology. The examination reveals the generator is irreparably damaged, establishing evacuation as the only victory condition.
   _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 4 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

7. **The Lie choice** *(story)* — Presented with two options: lie ("I know of another city") or tell the truth ("I don't know where to go"). **Do NOT lie.** Lying: Hope rises greatly but triggers the "Price of a Lie" blackmail arc and may force killing an engineer; the Execution Place, once built, cannot be dismantled. Telling the truth: work efficiency −10%. Branches the ending. PoNR: **YES**.
   _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 4 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: mainline · spoiler: story · missable: yes_

8. **Prepare the evacuation (~13 days)** *(progression)* — Build the Evacuation Centre; send initial 25 engineers (train arrives in 24h). The generator suffers intermittent malfunctions; assign engineers to the Repair Station. Guard Stations / Faith Keepers ease stowaway and riot events.
   _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 4 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

9. **Minimum evacuation threshold** *(progression)* — Must send ≥25 engineers, 250 food rations, 300 steel, 1 Steam Core, and 5000 coal to the Dreadnought to enable launch. PoNR: **YES** (can't launch below this).
   _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

10. **Full Dreadnought (best outcome)** *(late-game)* — Capacity 500. Full retrofit cost: **5000 Coal, 5000 Food Rations, 9000 Steel, 10 Steam Cores.** Partially upgraded decks cannot house people. Determines ending quality. → `endings/winterhome_endings.md`
    _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_

11. **Evacuation order** *(none)* — Children first, then workers, then engineers. Provide prostheses to amputees before sending.
    _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_

12. **Generator final malfunction (~day 26) / final 48 hours** *(late-game)* — After the final permanent malfunction there are ~2 days to launch (extendable ~1 day with 1 Steam Core + 5 workers at the Repair Station) before the generator explodes and the Dreadnought auto-launches. PoNR: **YES** (endgame).
    _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_

13. **Launch** *(story)* — Manual launch is possible any time after 5000 coal is supplied. Generator explosion triggers auto-launch. PoNR: **YES** (scenario end).
    _source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 (P2, 3 sources) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: mainline · spoiler: story_
