# Frostpunk -- Faith Path (Purpose Tree)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02

The **Faith** tree is one of the two mutually-exclusive **Purpose** branches (the other is Order). Purpose unlocks mid-scenario via a story trigger; once you sign the first Purpose law of either tree, the other tree is **permanently locked**. Faith focuses on raising Hope and delivering citizen benefits.

> Most of this file is `spoiler: late-game` / `story` -- it documents mid-to-endgame law consequences and ending-linked choices. At enemy-tier 0 / puzzle-tier 1 the deeper branch content is gated; the reader surfaces it when the player raises their tier or asks post-event.

## Branch chain

**Main line:** House of Prayer → Evening Prayers → **Faith Keepers** → Public Penance → **Protector of the Truth** → **New Faith**

**Side branches:** Shrines → Field Kitchens / House of Healing; The Temple → **Righteous Denunciation**

## "Crossing the line" laws
**Protector of the Truth** and **Righteous Denunciation** are the Faith-tree "crossing the line" laws. Signing either **blocks the good / Golden Path ending** in A New Home.
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` PON-002, DEP-004: signing either crossing-the-line law permanently locks out the Saviour ending and the Golden Path achievement.

## New Faith (terminal law)
The final law of the Faith branch. **Removes the Hope meter entirely** and **auto-builds an Execution Platform.** This is the authoritarian-Faith endgame state and is linked to the "Iron Saviour"-style ending outcomes (see `endings/a_new_home_endings.md`).
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: mainline · spoiler: story_
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-002: New Faith removes the Hope meter entirely; Hope-based management is no longer possible after signing it.

## For the good ending
To keep the **Saviour** (humanitarian) ending in A New Home, **stop before Protector of the Truth** -- the early Faith laws (House of Prayer, Evening Prayers, Faith Keepers, Shrines → House of Healing) are safe and provide strong Hope tools, which is why mainstream guides consider Faith the easier path through the Londoners crisis.
