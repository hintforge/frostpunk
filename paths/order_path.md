# Frostpunk -- Order Path (Purpose Tree)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02

The **Order** tree is one of the two mutually-exclusive **Purpose** branches (the other is Faith). Purpose unlocks mid-scenario via a story trigger; signing the first Purpose law of either tree **permanently locks** the other. Order focuses on lowering Discontent and boosting productivity through surveillance and discipline. **Under Order, the Hope bar is replaced by an Obedience bar.**

> Most of this file is `spoiler: late-game` / `story`. Gated at puzzle-tier 1.

## Branch chain

**Discipline line:** Neighbourhood Watch → Morning Gathering → **Foreman**

**Security line:** Guard Stations → Patrol

**Control line:** **Propaganda Centre** → Agitator / **Pledge of Loyalty**

**Coercion line:** **Prison** → **Forceful Persuasion** → **New Order**

## "Crossing the line" laws
**Pledge of Loyalty** and **Forceful Persuasion** are the Order-tree "crossing the line" laws. Signing either **blocks the good / Golden Path ending** in A New Home.
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` PON-002, DEP-004: signing either crossing-the-line law permanently locks out the Saviour ending and the Golden Path achievement.

## New Order (terminal law)
The final law of the Order branch. **Removes the Hope meter entirely** and **auto-builds an Execution Platform.** This is the authoritarian-Order endgame state, linked to the "Iron Saviour"-style ending outcomes (see `endings/a_new_home_endings.md`).
_source: Deep Research handoff 2026-06-02 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: mainline · spoiler: story_
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-002: New Order removes the Hope meter entirely (replaced by Obedience); Hope-based management is no longer possible after signing it.

## For the good ending
To keep the **Saviour** ending in A New Home, **stop before Pledge of Loyalty / Forceful Persuasion.** The early Order laws (Neighbourhood Watch → Guard Stations → Patrol, Propaganda Centre → Agitator) are safe; Foreman and Agitator notably boost workshop research, making Order the stronger pick when Discontent rather than Hope is your binding problem.
